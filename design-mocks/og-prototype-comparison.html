Recommendation

Build the signal-led poster.

Use the answer signal as the invariant structure and media as a controlled atmospheric layer. This preserves legibility at thumbnail size, keeps claims auditable, and still gives Red Thread a recognisable match-night presence. Archive styling should remain a special treatment for Stories—not the universal renderer.
